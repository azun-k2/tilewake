How to play

Tap a tile next to the gap, or a tile in the gap's row or column to slide the whole line, or swipe. A tile in its own place wakes up; put every tile home and the whole picture wakes. Hold Peek to see the finished picture; a quick tap keeps it shown until your next move.

Questions

How do stars and par work?

Each board has a par, a number of moves a good player can reach. Solve within par for three stars, within one and a half times par for two, and any solve earns one.

Why is a world asleep?

A world wakes after you solve three boards of the world before it. Its card says how far you are.

How do I unlock everything, or restore my purchase?

Open the Unlock screen from Home or any locked world. Restore purchase is on the same screen and in Settings; use it on a new device with the same store account.

The pictures are not moving.

Pictures hold still to save power: after 30 seconds without a touch, in Low Power Mode or Battery Saver, when the device is too warm, with Reduce Motion (or animations off on Android), or when Still pictures is on in Settings. Settings says which one applies.

The sounds are too loud or too quiet.

Settings › Sound has a switch and a volume for effects, music and picture sounds, and a choice of sound styles and music tracks.
