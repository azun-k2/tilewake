What it is

Tilewake is a sliding puzzle with living pictures. A tile in its own place wakes up and its piece of the scene starts to move; a tile out of place sleeps, still and grey. The last move wakes the whole picture.

  • A journey through 12 painted worlds, from a koi pond and a lantern river to a snowy village and an orbit in space, each with its own sounds and a finale when it wakes.
  • Boards from 3×3 to 5×5, with a par and up to three stars, and four more shuffles of every board to replay.
  • A daily puzzle with a streak, and a widget for it on iPhone, iPad and Android.
  • Free play on any size, with your own photos as the picture.
  • Peek at the whole picture, numbers on the tiles, and calm sound styles and music to choose from.

Free to play: the first two worlds, the daily puzzle and free play 3×3. One purchase unlocks every world, every size and your own photos. Requires iOS 17, macOS 14 Sonoma or Android 8 or later.

Privacy in one paragraph

Tilewake has no ads, no accounts of its own, no analytics and no tracking. Your progress and your photos stay on your device. The full privacy policy says the same at more length.
